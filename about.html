About MeiGaming

An independent software development studio focused on minimalism, speed, and privacy.

The Vision

MeiGaming (founded by Aravindh) was built on a simple premise: software should be fast, quiet, and elegant. In a world full of ad-cluttered and bloated applications, MeiGaming creates clean utilities designed to empower users without getting in their way.

Design & Craftsmanship

Inspired by Apple's human interface guidelines and traditional Japanese Zen aesthetic principles (Kanso - simplicity, Shibui - subtle beauty), every pixel and interaction is carefully considered.

  • Zero Bloat: Lightweight codebases engineered natively for maximum performance.
  • Privacy-First: Applications work offline whenever possible and collect zero unnecessary analytics.
  • Accessibility & Dark Mode: Seamless adaptation to user environment and preferences.
