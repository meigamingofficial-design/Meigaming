The Studio Vision

MeiGaming was founded on April 18, 2026 with a singular mission: to eliminate software bloat and restore elegance, speed, and privacy to everyday mobile and desktop tools.

Modern software is frequently burdened by intrusive ads, background tracking, and slow hybrid frameworks. MeiGaming takes the opposite approach — building purposeful, single-responsibility utilities engineered natively for peak efficiency and longevity.
