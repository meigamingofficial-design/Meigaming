About Mei Convertor

Mei Convertor is a powerful, offline file utility designed to handle document, image, PDF, and text conversions locally on your Android device. Styled with elegant Japanese cherry blossom (Sakura) aesthetics, it keeps your private files completely safe.

Key Features

  • 🖼️ Image Tools: Convert, compress, and resize JPG, PNG, WEBP, and format variations instantly.
  • 📄 PDF Tools: Convert, merge, split, and compress PDF documents with high fidelity.
  • 📝 Documents Suite: Seamless conversion across Word (.docx), Excel, PowerPoint (.pptx), and plain text.
  • ⚡ Quick Convert: Convert any file in a flash with a simple, intuitive tap interface.
  • 🔒 Secure & Private: 100% on-device processing. Your files stay private and never touch an external server.
