Description

Mei Convertor is an all-in-one offline file conversion app. Designed with privacy in mind, all conversions happen locally on your device without sending sensitive documents or images to cloud servers.

Key Features

  • 100% Offline Processing: Zero cloud reliance. Your files never leave your device.
  • Multi-Format Support: Convert PDFs, images (PNG, JPG, WEBP), text documents, and audio tracks.
  • Batch Conversion: Convert multiple files simultaneously in seconds.
  • Zero Quality Loss: High precision conversion engines for crisp vector and text rendering.

System Requirements

OS: Android 8.0 or higher
Storage: 25 MB free space
