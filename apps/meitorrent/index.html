About MeiTorrent

MeiTorrent is a modern, open-source Android torrent client built for speed, reliability, and simplicity. It allows you to download magnet links and .torrent files with seamless, stable background transfers.

Key Features

  • ⚡ Blazing Fast Performance: Powered by a native libtorrent C++ core for ultra-high download speeds.
  • 🛡️ Privacy First: 100% Private. Zero ads, zero tracking scripts, zero data collection.
  • 🧲 Magnet & .Torrent Support: Easily add magnet links or torrent files and manage downloads with full control.
  • 🔋 Background Ready: Optimized for stable background downloading with smart battery guidance and auto-pause.
  • 🎨 Minimalist Zen Aesthetics: Traditional Japanese art inspired interface with dark & light theme support.
